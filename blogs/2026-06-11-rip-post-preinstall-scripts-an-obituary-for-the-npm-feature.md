It is with a complicated mixture of grief, relief, and the quiet satisfaction of an "I told you so" held in for over a decade that we record the passing of automatic lifecycle script execution in npm, who died at home in package.json on 9 June 2026. The cause was a default change in npm v12, announced that morning. The deceased had been ailing for years and is not expected to be missed by anyone who has had to rotate a set of CI credentials at 2 am.
